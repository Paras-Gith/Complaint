What each status means

  1. Pending

    Your complaint is saved with its category, priority and the time you filed it. Nobody has started on it yet.

  2. In Progress

    An administrator has picked it up. Any remark they leave appears on the complaint page.

  3. Resolved

    The administrator has closed it. The complaint and its dated history stay under My Complaints.
